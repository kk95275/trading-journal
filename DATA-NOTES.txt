ERA SUMMARY
===========
2003-2005 : Sparse era. 73-83% 1-min density; quiet minutes often have no
            bar. Usable, but 1-min charts look "chunkier". Fine for 5m+.
2006-2011 : Very good. 93-99% density; solid for 1-min backtesting.
2012-2026 : Excellent. 99.7-99.9% density — effectively perfect 1-min
            data. Best era for precise 1-min entries/exits (14+ years).

GAPS = MARKET CLOSURES, NOT MISSING DATA
========================================
- Weekends: gold closes Fri ~21:58 GMT, reopens Sun ~22:00/23:00 GMT.
  All 1,195 weekend gaps match this pattern. Normal.
- Daily settlement break: ~1h gap every trading day around 21:58-23:00 GMT
  (recent years). Normal.
- US holidays / half-days: larger weekday gaps land exactly on Christmas
  Eve, New Year's Eve, Thanksgiving, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Memorial
  Day, July 4th, Labor Day. Normal.
- Fully missing weekdays: only 20 in 23 years, all market holidays
  (Good Friday, Christmas Day, New Year's Day):
  2012-04-06, 2013-03-29, 2013-12-25, 2014-04-18, 2015-04-03, 2015-12-25,
  2016-01-01, 2016-03-25, 2017-04-14, 2018-03-30, 2019-04-19, 2020-04-10,
  2020-12-25, 2021-01-01, 2021-04-02, 2021-12-24, 2022-04-15, 2023-04-07,
  2024-03-29, 2025-04-18
  => Zero real trading days missing.

KNOWN DEFECTS
=============
- Exactly one out-of-order timestamp: 2004-04-30 (a row jumps back to
  00:00 after 11:56). The app's import step drops/fixes this row.
  Only data error found in 7.77M rows.

IMPLICATIONS FOR THE APP
========================
- Store/display timestamps as GMT; no DST conversion needed at import.
- Prices are Bid: real spread means Ask entries fill slightly higher.
  Consider a configurable spread (e.g. 20-40 cents) applied to buys/SL/TP
  touches for realism in the replay engine.
- Replay skips market closures seamlessly (like TradingView replay);
  optional "market closed" marker when crossing a gap.
